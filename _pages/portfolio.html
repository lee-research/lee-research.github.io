Research · 2025–2026

Activation Boundary Matching

Task-Informed Initialization for Low-Rank Adaptation

How can early adaptation dynamics provide useful structure for initializing parameter-efficient models?

We found that activation boundaries stabilize earlier than activation values during adaptation. ABM-LoRA transfers these early-emerging boundaries from a short probe adapter to initialize LoRA, providing direct layer-wise supervision without additional inference overhead.

Under Review at AAAI 2027 · Co-first Author

Research · 2025

Information-Theoretic Representation Learning

Information Bottleneck and Invariant Representations

What information should a learned representation preserve, discard, or separate?

Studied Information Bottleneck and invariant information principles to characterize information retention in deep representations and distinguish task-relevant structure from spurious features. This work also motivated later investigation into which internal representational structures can be transferred during adaptation.

Independent Research · 2025

HI-LoRA

Hybrid Initialization for Low-Rank Adaptation

What information is lost when low-rank adaptation is initialized using only gradients or activation magnitude?

Designed a hybrid LoRA initialization strategy combining gradient-based and magnitude-based information to preserve both directional and scale information. The method improved early-training stability and reduced variance across random seeds.

Multimodal Learning · 2024

Noise-Robust Multimodal Recognition

Adaptive Lip Reading and Speech Recognition

How can visual and auditory information be combined adaptively when speech becomes unreliable under noise?

Led a five-person interdisciplinary team developing a multimodal recognition system that dynamically switches between lip reading and automatic speech recognition based on estimated noise levels. Built synthetic noisy training data and implemented a LipNet-based CNN-GRU pipeline in PyTorch.

Team Leader & Lead Researcher · Chung-Ang University
